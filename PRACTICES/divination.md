## Tarot Reading

### The Major Arcana
The twenty-two cards representing major life themes and spiritual lessons.

- **The Fool** - New beginnings, taking risks, faith in the journey
- **The Magician** - Manifestation, resourcefulness, willpower
- **The High Priestess** - Intuition, sacred knowledge, divine feminine
- **The Empress** - Abundance, fertility, creative energy
- **The Emperor** - Authority, structure, masculine leadership
- **The Hierophant** - Tradition, spirituality, belief systems

### The Minor Arcana
Fifty-six cards divided into four suits, representing daily influences and circumstances.

- **Cups** - Emotions, relationships, creativity
- **Wands** - Passion, energy, inspiration
- **Swords** - Intellect, conflict, clarity
- **Pentacles** - Material matters, work, earthly concerns

## Scrying

### Mirror Scrying
Gaze into a reflective surface to perceive visions and messages.

**Preparation:**
1. Cast a protective circle
2. Dim the lighting to shadows and candlelight
3. Focus on the center of the mirror
4. Allow your mind to open to impressions and imagery
5. Record what you perceive

### Crystal Ball Scrying
Using quartz spheres to access higher planes of consciousness.
