# 🌕 Full Moon Ceremony

*Amplifying Intention Under the Lunar Peak*

---

## Preparation

### Sacred Space Setup
1. Choose a quiet location, outdoors if possible
2. Create a circle of salt or stones
3. Place candles at cardinal directions (optional)
4. Gather your ritual tools and personal items

### Timing
- Perform 3 hours before or after exact full moon time
- Evening hours are traditionally most powerful
- Allow 30-60 minutes for complete ritual

### Items Needed
- White candle (represents lunar energy)
- Journal and pen
- Clear quartz crystal
- Salt or sage for cleansing
- Water bowl
- Personal intention items

---

## The Ritual

### Step 1: Grounding & Centering (5 min)
Stand with feet planted firmly on earth. Take three deep breaths:
- First breath: Release
- Second breath: Receive
- Third breath: Align

Feel the lunar energy descending through your crown chakra.

### Step 2: Circle Casting (3 min)
Walk counterclockwise, sprinkling salt:
*"I cast this sacred space, protected and purified, open only to energies of love and light."*

### Step 3: Candle Lighting (3 min)
Light candles at each direction, speaking:
- **East (Air)**: *"I welcome clarity and communication"*
- **South (Fire)**: *"I welcome transformation and will"*
- **West (Water)**: *"I welcome emotion and intuition"*
- **North (Earth)**: *"I welcome grounding and abundance"*

### Step 4: Moonlight Bathing (5 min)
Raise your arms to the moon. Feel lunar energy filling your being.
Visualize silver light penetrating every cell, every chakra.

### Step 5: Intention Release (10 min)
Write on paper or speak aloud:
- What you wish to amplify
- What energy you're calling in
- What you're ready to complete or release

### Step 6: Crystal Charging (5 min)
Hold crystal in moonlight:
*"Moon of power, charge this stone with your radiance. May it carry your light into my world."*

### Step 7: Gratitude Circle (3 min)
Place your hands on heart:
*"I am grateful for the moon's guidance, for the cycle's wisdom, for the divine feminine reflected within and without."*

### Step 8: Circle Closing (3 min)
Walk clockwise, saying:
*"The circle closes, the ritual ends, but the moon's blessing remains within me."*

---

## Post-Ritual Practice

### Integration
- Keep your written intention visible for the next 29 days
- Carry or wear your charged crystal
- Perform daily affirmations aligned with your intention
- Notice synchronicities appearing in your life

### Journaling Prompts
- What did you feel during the ceremony?
- What insights arose?
- How will you honor this lunar cycle?
- What commitments are you making?
